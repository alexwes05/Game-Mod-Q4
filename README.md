My mod is a fishing simulator.

To play the mod copy and paste the pak file over to a new folder named "Fishing Simulator" or whatever you would like to call the mod.
Once you launch quake there should be a mods tab and click on it and there is where you can swap into it.

First you have to press h and it tells you all what you have to do in game to get it to work.
The fishing mechanic is pretty self explanatory then there is 5 different fishing perks you can get to boost your fishing skills.
Examples include increasing rarity of fish caught, increasing number of fish caught at once, and increasing bait can hold at once.
Warning: there is a chance you can reel in monsters be careful.
